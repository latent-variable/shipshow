[warm, hands-on, thinking out loud with the viewer] Let's try. Spin the needle around its middle, like a propeller. It sweeps a disk: about 0.79 square units, if the needle is one unit long. Now spin it around one end instead. Worse. A disk four times bigger, about 3.14. So the middle wins?

[building to a small surprise, a little delighted] Not so fast. Try a three point turn, like turning a car around in a narrow street. Slide, turn, slide, turn. The needle traces this curved triangle, called a deltoid, and it takes half the area of the propeller. That was Sōichi Kakeya's question, in 1917, and for a while this looked like the answer.
