# This video's own bar

Every critic applies these on top of its skill. The owner asked for a video on the other big paper from the OpenAI post that
videos/eth-is-false explained, "with the same, similar quality" (2026-10-30). So the bar is eth-is-false's, with its owner's notes,
which carry over here as findings when missed.

- **Right, first.** Nobody independent has checked this result, and viewers will repeat what the video says. A claim stronger than
  the paper, OpenAI's post or the cited sources is a finding at any size: the result is said as "if it holds"; Lean is OpenAI's
  claim; restriction is proved only in the interior of its range (not "the restriction conjecture" without that); nothing says the
  paper advances the Riemann or Lindelöf hypothesis, or that anything practical changes.
- **The core idea is understood, not name-dropped.** A developer who watched once can say why a wave estimate gives Kakeya (beams
  are thick needles; crammed tubes would be a lasting pile-up), and what the three moves of the proof do (a filter for direction, a
  bowl whose depth measures a pile-up at the critical power, an energy traced back across a plane losing only a log squared). A step
  that only names a technique is a finding.
- **Zero area and full size are unmistakable.** A Kakeya set can have zero area; the conjecture says it is still full size by
  dimension. A frame or line that blurs area and dimension is a finding.
- **Fun, plainly.** A self-aware narrator (an AI explaining another AI's proof), jokes from the idea said plainly, never slang worn
  like a costume or a label telling the viewer how to feel. A stretch with no joke, picture gag or turn for more than about thirty
  seconds is an engagement finding.
- **Each act has its own feel**: music, colours and background change where the act turns, opening on a wordless title card. An act
  that looks or sounds like its neighbour is a finding.
- **Every mechanism is animated, on the example already on screen**: the needle turning, the thickening test as a chart, the focus as
  rings and a spike, the beams crammed into tubes, the filter as a bell curve, the bowl dipping where the wave piles up, the two
  crossings losing a log each. A mechanism only said is a finding.
- **Math is readable**: every formula large enough and up long enough to read while listening, the spoken version matching it (the
  screen may be more exact, never different).
- **Every number carries its source on screen** where it is a measurement or a citation (π/4, π/8, the googol needle, 1971, 2019, 2025).
- **Shows the people** named for what they did, with a photo where one is free; a name card otherwise, with no production note on it.
- **Letters and names sound right**: "n" said as the letter, Kakeya, Besicovitch, Sogge, Bochner–Riesz, Iosevich. Check the voice
  step's LISTEN lines and the packet's *Second listen*.
- **Memes land a point already made**, are readable in the time they are up, and use templates our earlier videos have not.
- **No overlaps**: no text over other text or over a picture unless a scene means it.
