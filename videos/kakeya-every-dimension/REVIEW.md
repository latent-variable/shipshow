## Round 0: the script alone, before any voice

Two fresh critics read the script and sources before GPU time was spent on it.

| Critic | Model | Verdict | Blocking / major / minor |
|---|---|---|---|
| accuracy | Opus | fail | 0 / 2 / 6 |
| clarity + engagement | Sonnet | fail | 0 / 12 / 8 |

What changed:
- **Restriction overclaimed (major).** 04b's "each level implies the one below" and 06c's "the whole tower" let a viewer conclude the
  restriction conjecture is proved; the paper gets its interior only. 04b now says "give or take some edge cases", 06c says
  "Bochner Riesz, most of restriction, and Kakeya", and 07 says "except a thin edge of the restriction block, which stays open".
- **Tao misattributed (major).** "Kakeya was never really about needles ... Tao puts it this way" put a framing in his mouth. 07 now
  attributes only what his survey says: cramming tubes is central to problems about how much waves pile up.
- **Accuracy minors.** The thickening test is now about the rate ("no power of the thickness keeps up"), the log loss "fits inside what
  the conjecture allows" (log squared is not claimed optimal), the near-balance is in time and the two extra time directions, tubes
  "can't be crammed together by much", the model is "unnamed internal" and Lean ran "the day after the model finished", and the bowl's
  "curvature" became "bend", with sources.md naming the background it sits on.
- **Clarity.** "Power" is defined in 04 (the wave raised to p; a higher p counts a tall spike more). 05 says why a crammed bundle piles
  up (overlapping strengths add) and ties back to the thickening test; the random signs are a trailing technicality. 06c is three
  short beats: balance, the walker between two circles, two crossings for two logs. 06b says why the bowl gives the critical power
  (depth grows with density, so depth times wave counts a dense pile-up extra). 06 says what the imaginary shift does (a wiggle turns
  into a fade that depends on direction) and cuts the flow to a clause. 02b marks the turning versus laying-down contrast out loud.
  03 gives the like-for-like number (a line that thin: a googolth). 05b drops Bourgain, Demeter and decoupling. 07b ties Hausdorff
  dimension to "full size". "OpenAI's collection" became "an earlier OpenAI paper".
- **Engagement.** Seven memes now, none used by an earlier video: One Does Not Simply (02b), Vince McMahon (03, the googol), Scooby
  Doo Reveal (04b, the needle problem was a wave problem), Epic Handshake (05), Hide the Pain Harold (06b), Phoebe Teaching Joey
  (06c, the recap walked through, replacing Midwit, which would have read as mocking the proof), Khaby Lame (07b). A self-aware joke
  in 06 ("if that sounds like something an AI would make up ..."), the book-club joke now sets itself up, 02 opens on the table and
  04 on a question, and "here's the beautiful part", "brutally hard" and "Math is weird like that" are gone.
- **Declined.** The critic's "stretched sheet with weights" for the bowl: that picture is a linear equation (the paper's Appendix D
  uses one only in two dimensions), and it would teach the wrong reason for the critical power, which comes from the determinant.
  The engagement critic's "a bridge shakes apart" for 04: not in any source.
