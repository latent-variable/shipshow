[matter-of-fact] The hard part is three knobs at once: how many slopes at each point, how many notes to try, and how big each group gets.
[dry] Smaller groups and more points each help, but both make the notes bigger and slower to try. Turn one knob, and another one bites.
[clear] Balance all three, and the exponent drops to roughly n over the square root of log n.
