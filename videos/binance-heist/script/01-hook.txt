[urgent and gripping, like the cold open of a heist film told to a friend, pulling them close] Thursday, October twenty ninth. A little after eight in the evening, universal time, money started pouring out of Binance, the biggest crypto exchange in the world. Bitcoin, ether, digital dollars, on ten blockchains at once.

[stunned, letting a huge number land slowly] About twenty minutes later, most of six point three billion dollars was gone.

[incredulous, raising the stakes, then turning to a surprise] The biggest crypto theft ever recorded. Roughly four times the old record. The FBI says it was North Korea. And then, within the hour, something you'd think crypto can't do: the first of it froze. Within hours, about half was stuck in place, like a car with a clamp on the wheel.
