[urgent and gripping, like the cold open of a heist film told to a friend, pulling them close] Thursday, October twenty ninth. A little after eight in the evening, universal time, money started pouring out of Binance, the biggest crypto exchange in the world. Bitcoin, ether, digital dollars, on ten blockchains at once. About twenty minutes later, most of six point three billion dollars was gone.

[incredulous, raising the stakes, then turning to a surprise] The biggest crypto theft ever recorded. Roughly four times the old record. The FBI says it was North Korea. And then, within the hour, something you'd think crypto can't do: about half of it was frozen. Stuck in place, like a car with a clamp on the wheel.
