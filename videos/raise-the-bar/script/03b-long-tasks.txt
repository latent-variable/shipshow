[thoughtful, working through an example by hand, inviting the viewer along] Why does length matter so much? Take a task with a hundred steps, where one wrong step sinks the whole thing. A model that gets each step right ninety nine times in a hundred finishes about a third of these tasks. A model that's right ninety seven times in a hundred, nearly as good, finishes about one in twenty.

[letting it land, then plain and certain] Two points apart on every step. Nearly eight times apart at the finish. Long tasks don't create a gap. They magnify one.
