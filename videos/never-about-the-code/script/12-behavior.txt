You don't need to understand every line. You need to understand the behavior. What does it do for the person using it? What must it never do? Leak a customer's data, for one. And how do you know? A test that fails when it breaks, or you watched it work. Karpathy says it too. Don't tell it what to do. Give it success criteria, and watch it go. And when it surprises you, drop down a rung and read the code. The way you'd open the assembly. Once in a while, when something's really wrong.
