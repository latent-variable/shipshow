Exhibit A. Grace Hopper. Navy officer. Compiler pioneer. Her team once logged a real moth as an actual bug. In 1952 she had a working compiler, and nobody would touch it. Computers only do arithmetic, they told her. Exhibit B. John Backus, the guy who built Fortran. He said programmers back then saw themselves as a priesthood. A machine writing efficient code? A foolish and arrogant dream.
