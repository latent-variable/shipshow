And the people who doubted it are switching. In October 2025, Andrej Karpathy said you couldn't hand agents real work yet, because they just don't work. Three months later, he'd flipped. Eighty percent manual coding became eighty percent agent coding. DHH, who created Ruby on Rails, said that a year before, he didn't like the AI on offer. Then Opus 4.5 was the dividing line. Antirez, who created Redis, thought we had more time before programming changed completely. Now he writes that for most projects, writing the code yourself no longer makes sense, unless you're doing it for fun.
