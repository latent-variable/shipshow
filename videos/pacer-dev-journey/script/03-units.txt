Pacing comes down to one line. It compares spent, which the ledger keeps in USD cents, with the daily budget. The budget is in the campaign's own minor units. For dollars, those match. A euro budget worth a hundred dollars, nine thousand two hundred, is read as ninety-two, so euro stops early. Yen have no minor unit. Fifteen thousand yen is a hundred dollars, but pacing reads it as fifteen thousand cents, a hundred fifty dollars. With batching off, Tokyo alone stops at one hundred fifty percent. That is the first cause.
